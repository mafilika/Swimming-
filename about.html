About R&R Swimming Pools, Plumbing & Building

At R&R Swimming Pools and Projects, we specialize in creating luxury swimming pools and pool solutions that transform your backyard into a personal oasis. From new pool installations to renovations, repairs, and maintenance, our team delivers expert craftsmanship and attention to detail in every project. Whether you dream of a modern pool, a family-friendly retreat, or a sleek spa-style design, we provide customized solutions that fit your home, lifestyle, and budget. Serving Cape Town and Stradfontein we ensure a seamless, stress-free experience from start to finish.

Our Mission

R&R Swimming Pools and Projects is committed to transforming outdoor spaces across Cape Town into luxurious, functional, and value-enhancing living environments. We specialize in custom swimming pool construction, pool renovations, outdoor entertainment areas, paving, decking, and professional building services designed to meet the highest standards of craftsmanship and durability. Our mission is to educate homeowners on quality construction practices, premium materials, and long-term pool maintenance solutions — ensuring every swimming pool and outdoor project is structurally sound, aesthetically superior, and built to withstand Cape Town’s coastal climate. Through expert design, precise engineering, and transparent project management, we deliver high-end swimming pools, outdoor renovations, and residential building projects that increase property value and exceed client expectations.
